WHO I AM

A bit about me

Hi, I'm Rushikesh — a Business Analyst based in Chennai, passionate about working at the intersection of product strategy, data, and delivery. I thrive in SaaS environments where understanding the "why" behind user problems leads to better product decisions.

Over the past 2+ years, I've worked closely with product managers, engineers, and business stakeholders to gather requirements, write user stories, map processes, and track delivery across fast-moving sprints. I believe great analysis isn't just about diagrams and documentation — it's about asking the right questions.

When I'm not untangling requirements or running sprint ceremonies, I'm probably exploring a new SaaS tool, reading about product frameworks, or improving my workflow with automation.
